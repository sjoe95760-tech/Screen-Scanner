# Screen-Scanner

A simple program that lets you scan QR codes directly from your PC screen. No need to use your mobile phone to scan a QR from your desktop!

## 🚀 How to Download & Run
1. Look at the **right side** of this page and click on **Releases** (or click the "Releases" tab on the right).
2. Click on **v1.0**.
3. Under "Assets", click on **`app.exe`** to download it.
4. Double-click the downloaded `.exe` file to run the app. (No installation or Python required!).

*(Note: Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning because it's a new app. Just click **More info** -> **Run anyway**).*

## 📖 How to Use
1. Open the app and click **Start**.
2. Press **F8** anywhere on your keyboard.
3. Drag a box around any QR code on your screen.
4. The app will automatically open the link or show you the text!

## 🛠️ Built With
* Python
* CustomTkinter (UI)
* OpenCV & Pyzbar (QR Scanning)
